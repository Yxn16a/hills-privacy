Privacy Policy for Hills

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Hills Systems (“Hills,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Hills mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, retain, and protect information when you use Hills.

1. Information we collect

Account and contact information

When you create or use an account, we collect information such as your name, phone number, email address if you provide it, account role, and account identifier. Hills uses a phone number and password to create and access an account. Passwords are used for authentication and are not displayed to other Hills users.

Shop, profile, and other content you provide

We collect content you choose to submit through Hills, including profile photos; shop, product, service, event, or business-listing information; photos; feedback; reviews; support requests; task notes; and other free-form content. We use this information to provide the feature for which it was submitted and to operate Hills.

Shop owners may also choose to record or upload short Social videos. A Social video may include the video image, accompanying sound, a cover image, caption, product or shop association, and viewing or reporting information. We use this content to provide, moderate, secure, and measure the Social-video feature.

Customer requests and responses

Hills lets users send requests to shops, such as availability, reservation, price, deal, or discount requests. We collect the request text, related product and shop information, and any response supplied by the shop owner or authorized Hills staff. These requests and responses are made available to the relevant shop, requester, and authorized Hills personnel so they can be handled.

Sales, customer, and payout information

Shop owners may optionally use Hills’ Sales Book to record business sales, credits, expenses, products, amounts, payment status or method, notes, and a customer’s name or phone number when the owner enters it. We use this information to provide the Sales Book and related business reporting features.

For agent compensation, authorized Hills staff may record payout amounts, payout status, payment references, and a payment destination or a masked payment-account detail for an agent. We use this information to administer, verify, and record agent compensation. We do not collect payment-card numbers through Hills.

Precise and approximate location

Hills does not continuously track users or collect location in the background.

An approved Hills agent may choose to use the Capture GPS pin feature while completing an assigned shop-location task. Only after the agent intentionally starts that feature and grants device permission, Hills may collect the device’s precise or approximate location, including latitude, longitude, and reported accuracy. The agent can decline the permission and can choose not to use the feature.

When a GPS pin is saved, Hills sends it to our servers and stores it with the relevant shop task and shop record. We use it to verify, correct, and provide the shop’s location and directions, and to support authorized operational review. We do not use precise location for advertising or marketing.

App activity, diagnostics, and identifiers

We collect information about how Hills is used, such as app interactions, in-app searches, views, follows, requests, feedback, and other actions taken in the app. We may also collect crash logs, diagnostic and performance information, app version, device language or locale, network status, notification-token information, and device or other identifiers. We use this information to provide, secure, troubleshoot, measure, and improve Hills.

Information stored on your device

Hills may store information locally on your device, such as sign-in tokens, language and onboarding preferences, selected search settings, and limited app state. Removing the app or signing out may remove some locally stored information, but does not by itself delete information already held on our servers.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • provide, maintain, and improve Hills and its shop-discovery, account, and business features;
  • create and manage accounts, authenticate users, and protect account security;
  • publish and maintain shop and product information submitted through the app;
  • provide Social videos, customer requests and responses, Sales Book, and agent-compensation features;
  • complete agent-assigned shop-location work when an agent chooses to capture a GPS pin;
  • respond to support requests and feedback;
  • diagnose crashes, prevent abuse, secure our services, and comply with applicable law; and
  • analyze app usage and performance to improve reliability and usability.

We do not sell personal information or use precise location for advertising or marketing.

3. How information is shared

Hills does not sell user data or share it with third parties for their independent advertising, marketing, or profiling purposes.

We use service providers that process information on our behalf and under our instructions, such as hosting, infrastructure, communications, and technical-support providers. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect the security and integrity of Hills, or to enforce our rights.

Content that a user intentionally posts or sends through a Hills feature may be displayed to the relevant Hills users or the public where that feature requires it. For example, shop-listing details, approved Social videos, shop reviews, customer requests and responses, and shop-location pins may be displayed to provide listings, maps, directions, or related functionality. A pin saved by an agent is used as the shop’s location; Hills does not display the agent’s live location.

4. Data retention

We retain account information and content for as long as needed to operate Hills, meet the purposes described in this Policy, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent fraud or abuse, and comply with legal obligations. We may retain or use de-identified or aggregated information where permitted by law.

If you request account deletion, we delete or de-identify account data and associated personal information unless we need to retain limited information for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or recordkeeping purposes. Details and instructions are available at Hills account deletion.

5. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain uses of your personal information.

You can:

  • update certain account information in Hills;
  • decline or revoke location permission in your device settings;
  • choose not to use the optional GPS-pin feature;
  • request deletion of your Hills account and associated data using the account deletion page; and
  • contact us with privacy questions or requests.

Revoking location permission prevents future device-location collection. It does not automatically remove a shop pin already saved to Hills; contact us or use the deletion process if you need help with a saved record.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. In production, Hills transmits user data over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children’s privacy

Hills is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Hills, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated Policy and revise the effective date above. Your continued use of Hills after an update is subject to the updated Policy, to the extent permitted by law.
